About Us

Vision: To empower marginalized communities by providing access to education, healthcare, livelihood opportunities, and social inclusion.

Leadership: Directed by Anil Ranjan and Ravi Sharma.

Values: Transparency, inclusivity, sustainability, and empowerment.

Our Programs

Cow Protection & Welfare

  • Protecting and rescuing cow-progeny from sickness and starvation
  • Providing shelter, feeding, and medical care in dedicated Gaushalas
  • Developing and improving indigenous (Bhartiya) cattle breeds

Cow-Based Economy & Dairy

  • Procuring and processing fresh local A2 milk directly from rural farmers
  • Manufacturing traditional Panchagavya medicines from dung, urine, and milk
  • Producing high-quality organic compost and bio-technical manures

Education Initiatives

  • Literacy programs for children and adults
  • Skill development workshops
  • Scholarships and study material distribution

Health & Wellness

  • Free medical camps and healthcare checkups
  • Preventive healthcare campaigns
  • Mental health, traditional wellness, and counseling support

Community Empowerment

  • Women empowerment projects
  • Youth leadership programs
  • Rural development and livelihood support

Social Welfare & Inclusion

  • Support for marginalized and tribal communities
  • Disaster relief and rehabilitation operations
  • Environmental awareness and eco-village initiatives
